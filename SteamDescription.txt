Unofficial performance and bug fixes for [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1414302321]Numbers[/url]. Reduces the lag while a Numbers tab is open, especially with expensive stat columns such as melee DPS, and fixes the prisoner interaction column.

[h2]Header menus and count[/h2][b]Cause:[/b] Numbers redraws its window several times per frame. Every time, it builds and sorts every drop-down menu at the top ([i]Stats[/i], [i]Health[/i], [i]Abilities[/i], [i]Other[/i]...), although a menu is only needed when its button is clicked, and it counts every pawn and corpse on the map for the [i]Count[/i] label.

[b]Fix:[/b] Menus are built only when you click a button; the menus you get are the same. The count is refreshed twice per second.

[h2]Text and icon cells[/h2][b]Cause:[/b] Every text cell (stats, records, capacities, jobs...) and icon cell (health, treatment...) is recalculated on every redraw. Stats are requested without any cache, so a column like melee DPS recalculates every pawn's attacks several times per frame.

[b]Fix:[/b] Cell values (including need-bar arrows and columns added by other mods) are cached for about half a second, and not recalculated at all while the game is paused. Outdated cells keep their last value and are recalculated oldest first, averaging at most about 1 ms per frame, so expensive columns such as melee DPS are spread out instead of causing a stutter (they may lag a few seconds behind while the game runs). A click or key press refreshes all cells, oldest first.

[h2]Sorting by a stat column[/h2][b]Cause:[/b] [i]PawnColumnWorker_Stat.Compare[/i] calculates the stat for both pawns on every comparison, so a sort calculates each pawn's stat many times.

[b]Fix:[/b] Each pawn's value is calculated once per sort. The resulting order is the same. With very expensive stats there is still a short pause, since every pawn's value is needed once.

[h2]Tooltips[/h2][b]Cause:[/b] Skill and disease-progression cells build their full tooltip text, and gear and inventory cells build every item's label, on every redraw, even when the mouse is not over them.

[b]Fix:[/b] The tooltip is built only for the cell under the mouse.

[h2]Prisoner interaction column[/h2][b]Bug:[/b] Numbers shows [i]Hemogen farm[/i] and [i]Bloodfeed only[/i] as radio buttons, but in vanilla they are independent options that can be combined with the others. Clicking them did nothing.

[b]Fix:[/b] They are now checkboxes (drag to toggle several prisoners), like in the vanilla prisoner tab. Enabling hemogen farming also adds or removes the hemogen extraction bill, as vanilla does.

[h2]Untranslated labels[/h2][b]Bug:[/b] In Traditional Chinese the bottom bar button stays [i]Numbers[/i], and a table saved as default is listed as [i]MainTable (Default)[/i] in the load menu and the mod settings, because Numbers builds that name from the internal def name.

[b]Fix:[/b] The button and default table names are translated (for example [i]殖民者（預設）[/i]). Other languages are unchanged, and saved layouts are not modified.

[h2]Notes[/h2][list]
[*]Interactive columns (inventory, equipment, drop buttons) still update instantly; only their tooltips are built lazily.
[*]With Development mode on, the mod logs the most expensive Numbers columns every 10 seconds.
[*]Requires Numbers. Load after Numbers.
[*]Safe to add to or remove from an existing save.
[/list]
